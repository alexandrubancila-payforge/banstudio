## Freelancer vs. agency

Both options have their advantages. The choice comes down to budget, complexity and preference.

**A freelancer** — ideal for small and medium projects. Direct communication, more accessible pricing, flexibility. The downside: one person, so capacity is limited.

**An agency** — a good fit for large projects or corporations. A dedicated team, standardised processes. The downside: higher prices, communication through a project manager, and sometimes a slower pace.

For most small and medium businesses in Moldova and Romania, an experienced freelancer is the best value for money.

## WordPress or custom code?

**WordPress** is a good call when:
- You need to update content frequently
- The budget is limited
- You want an active blog
- You need standard functionality (a form, a gallery, a portfolio)

**Custom code** (Astro, Next.js, HTML/CSS) is better when:
- Performance is critical (PageSpeed 95+)
- You want a 100% unique design
- Security is a priority
- The site doesn't need frequent content updates

## What to ask before hiring

1. **Do you have a portfolio?** — Ask for examples of projects similar to yours.
2. **Which technologies do you use?** — Make sure they are modern and appropriate.
3. **What does the price include?** — Design, development, hosting, basic SEO?
4. **How long will it take?** — A presentation website: 2-4 weeks. Something complex: 1-3 months.
5. **Do you offer post-launch support?** — Bugs, updates, small changes.

## Red flags

Watch out for:
- Suspiciously low prices (under €100 for a "professional" website)
- No portfolio and no references
- Promises like "page 1 on Google, guaranteed"
- Working exclusively with page builders (Elementor, Divi) with no knowledge of code
- No mention of performance, SEO or mobile

## Conclusion

Pick someone who understands not just the code, but your business too. A good web developer doesn't just build a website — they help you reach your business goals.
