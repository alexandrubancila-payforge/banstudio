"How much does a website cost?" is the first question I get — and it has the most annoying answer: **it depends**. But I won't leave it at that. Below you will find real price ranges for the Moldovan market in 2026, what exactly pushes the cost up or down, and how to avoid the cheap-website trap that costs you twice within a year.

## Indicative prices per type of site

The figures below are for a site that is **built professionally** (unique design, clean code, optimised) — not a template thrown together in an afternoon.

- **Landing page (single page)** — €150–€400. Ideal for a campaign, a single service or an event: hero, benefits, contact form.
- **Presentation website (4–8 pages)** — €400–€1,200. The most requested one: home, services, about, portfolio, contact, blog. Right for most small businesses.
- **Manageable WordPress website** — €500–€1,500. For when you want to edit the content yourself. With a custom theme, not a slow page builder.
- **Online shop (e-commerce)** — €1,000–€4,000+. Catalogue, cart, payments, shipping, accounts. The cost grows with the number of features.

> These are professional production prices. You will also find €50 offers — and the section below is precisely about those.

## What drives the price

Two "presentation websites" can be three times apart in price. Here is why:

1. **The design** — a ready-made template versus a unique design built around your business.
2. **The number of pages and features** — a simple form versus bookings, calculators, integrations.
3. **The content** — do you already have the copy and photos, or do they need to be created?
4. **Performance and SEO** — an optimised site (fast, with a [PageSpeed score of 100](/en/blog/pagespeed-100-how-i-got-a-perfect-score) and local SEO) takes more work, but it brings clients.
5. **Multilingual** — every extra language means extra content and extra structure.

## Why "cheap" ends up expensive

A €50–€100 website looks like a bargain. In practice, most of the time it means:

- a generic template that 500 other companies also use;
- slow loading → Google ranks you low and visitors leave;
- no SEO → nobody finds you;
- messy code → every change costs extra.

The result: a few months later you pay a second time to have it rebuilt. A good website is an **investment that brings clients**, not a box to tick.

## How to get a fair price

- Ask for a **detailed** quote, not just a number: which pages, which features, what is included (hosting, domain, maintenance).
- Ask whether the design is **unique** or a template.
- Ask for examples from the [portfolio](/en/portfolio) and check their speed on [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/).
- Clarify what happens **after** launch: who makes the changes, and how quickly.

## In short

For a small business in Moldova, a professional presentation website usually falls between **€400 and €1,200**, depending on design, pages and features. What matters is not being the cheapest, but **bringing you clients** and not costing you twice.
