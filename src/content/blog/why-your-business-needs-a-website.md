## Your clients are searching for you on Google

More than 80% of consumers search online before buying a product or a service. If your business doesn't show up on Google, you simply don't exist for those people.

A professional website is not just a digital business card — it is the single most important marketing tool your business has. It works 24/7, it doesn't ask for a salary, and it reaches clients you would have no other way of contacting.

## What you lose without a website

**Credibility.** The first question a prospective client asks: "Do they have a website?" If you don't, you look unprofessional — even when your service is excellent.

**New clients.** Every day without a website is a day when clients look for you, fail to find you, and go to the competition.

**Control.** On social media you are at the mercy of the algorithm. Your website is your property — you control the message, the design and the experience.

## Not every website brings results

A badly built website can do more harm than good. If it loads slowly, looks dated or isn't optimised for mobile, visitors leave within seconds.

What actually matters:

- **Speed** — under 3 seconds to load
- **Modern design** — first impressions count enormously
- **Mobile-first** — 70%+ of traffic comes from phones
- **SEO** — being found on Google, not merely existing
- **A clear call to action** — the visitor has to know what to do next

## What a professional website costs

It depends on complexity. A simple presentation website starts at a few hundred euros. A complex site with e-commerce can run into the thousands. But the investment pays itself back quickly when the site is built properly.

The important thing is not to confuse a low price with real savings. A €50 Wix site that brings in no clients is more expensive than a €500 site that generates sales.

## Conclusion

If you run a business and don't have a professional website, you are losing money every day. It doesn't have to be complicated — but it does have to be done right.
