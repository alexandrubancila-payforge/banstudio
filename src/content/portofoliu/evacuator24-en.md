## About the project

Evacuator24 provides round-the-clock towing and car transport in Chisinau and across Moldova. People look for a tow truck in stressful moments — the car won't start, there has been an accident, or they are stuck on the road — so the website had to do one thing very well: get the visitor to a phone call within seconds.

## What I built

- **One-tap calling** — the phone number is the first thing in the hero and stays visible in the top bar, with separate WhatsApp and Viber buttons
- **A dedicated page for every service**: towing, recovery of damaged cars, minibus and SUV recovery, car unlocking, jump starts, fuel delivery, and car transport to Romania, Germany, Bulgaria and Hungary
- **38 town pages** — tow truck in Balti, Cahul, Orhei, Ungheni, Tiraspol and more — each with its own copy about the area and the routes served, not one paragraph copied over
- **Prices shown openly**, from 300 lei for passenger cars, on a dedicated pricing page
- **Bilingual Romanian / Russian** — 114 pages in total, with hreflang and a separate sitemap per language
- **Structured data** for Google: AutomotiveBusiness, services with price offers, and FAQs

## Technology

A static site generated by a custom Node.js script, with no framework and no dependencies — clean HTML, optimised images and very fast loading even on mobile data, which is exactly where it gets used.
