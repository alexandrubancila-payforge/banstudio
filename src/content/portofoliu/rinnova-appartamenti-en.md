## About the project

Rinnova Appartamenti is a company from Verona, Italy, specialising in renovating and transforming residential spaces with quality finishes.

## What I built

- **A website entirely in Italian**
- **A visual portfolio** with before/after renovation projects
- **Services presented** — full renovation, finishes, interior design
- **A contact form** for quote requests
- **A premium design** that reflects the quality of the work

## The challenge

My first international project — I worked remotely with the client in Italy, communicating in Italian. I adapted the tone and the structure of the content to the Italian market.
