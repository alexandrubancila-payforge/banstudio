## About the project

Darhi brings together everything a Romanian company needs in one place — from incorporation, amendments and dissolution to accounting, legal work, funding and environmental compliance. The client works through a network of specialist partners, and the site had to bring order to a very broad offering.

## What I built

- **Twelve service lines**, each with its own detail page: accounting and tax advice, financial audit, legal services, health & safety and fire prevention, HR administration and recruitment, non-repayable funding, business loans, debt recovery, environmental compliance (AFM), IT services, company formation and amendments
- **The lead offer in the hero** — company formation at 750 lei, with the fee and the state charges listed separately, no hidden costs
- **A single path to conversion**: "Request a service" sits in the menu, in the hero and at the end of every service page
- **Dedicated sections** for how it works, partners and resources, so visitors understand the process before they ask for a quote
- **Contact details always visible** in the top bar: address, email and phone
- **Built on WordPress**, with a structure the team can extend with new services on their own

## Result

An offering with more than ten different directions becomes navigable: a visitor finds the right service in two steps and lands in a single request form.
