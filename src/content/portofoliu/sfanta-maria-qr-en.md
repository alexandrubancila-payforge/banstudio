## About the project

Sfânta Maria is a charity foundation in Chisinau. Its QR code goes on posters, leaflets and events, and whoever scans it should be able to do two things as easily as possible: find out who the foundation is, and donate.

## What we built

- **A donation screen**: bank details for the lei account and the euro account, with a copy button next to every line and one button that copies everything at once
- **The payment reference ready to copy**, so the donation reaches the foundation's books correctly
- **Every channel the foundation uses**: website, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Telegram, WhatsApp and a call button, plus the opening hours
- **Romanian and Russian**, switched with one tap
- **Adds to the phone's home screen** like an app, with the foundation's icon
- **Linked to the FP GROUP card**, the group the foundation belongs to

## Details

A single HTML page with no framework, in the blue of the logo, hosted on Cloudflare at qr.sfantamaria.md.
