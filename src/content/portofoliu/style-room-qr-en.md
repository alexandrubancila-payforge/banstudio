## About the project

Style Room is a barbershop on Mihail Kogălniceanu Street in Chisinau. Customers scan the QR code at the front desk, on the mirror or on a flyer and land on a single page made for the phone, where they can call, message on WhatsApp, check the prices or get directions.

## What we built

- **Everything on one screen**: the address, opening hours, a call button and links to the website, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Google Maps
- **A price list** on its own screen, with photos that can be zoomed
- **Romanian and Russian**, switched with one tap
- **Adds to the phone's home screen** like an app, with the barbershop's icon and separate instructions for iPhone and Android
- **Sharing and a QR code inside the card**: anyone can pass the link on or show the QR code to someone next to them
- **Brand colours**: the green and gold of the logo on a dark background

## Details

A single HTML page with no framework, hosted on Cloudflare at qr.styleroom.md. It opens instantly, even on a weak signal.
