## About the project

AnonXmd offers professional ECU tuning and automotive remapping services for every make. The site presents the full range: ECU Stage Remap, DPF/EGR/AdBlue off, DSG Torque Unlock, BMW FSC codes and much more.

## What I built

- **A modern dark design** with neon accents — a match for the automotive/tech field
- **A multilingual site** (EN/RO/RU) with a built-in language switcher
- **Detailed service pages** with prices, tags and imagery
- **A software page** listing the available tools and solutions
- **A contact form** with service selection and a price estimate
- **SEO optimisation** with meta tags, Schema.org and a sitemap
- **Animations and effects** — counter stats, fade-in, parallax

## Technologies

Custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript, responsive design, Google Fonts (Inter + JetBrains Mono), CSS animations, SEO optimised.
