## About the project

A passenger and parcel transport service on the Moldova — Germany — Netherlands — Belgium route. The client needed an online presence to reach customers in the diaspora.

## What I built

- **Routes presented** with maps and stop-off cities
- **Transparent pricing** per destination
- **Click-to-call** and WhatsApp for fast booking
- **SEO** optimised for the searches the Moldovan diaspora actually makes
