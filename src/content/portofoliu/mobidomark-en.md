## About the project

Mobidomark specialises in designing and manufacturing bespoke furniture — from 3D drawings through to final installation. Kitchens, wardrobes and living room furniture.

## What I built

- **A visual portfolio** of completed projects, organised by category
- **The process explained** — from measuring to installation
- **A services page** with technical detail
- **A quote request form** with purpose-built fields (dimensions, preferred material)
- **A photo gallery** showing the quality of the detailing

## Approach

A clean design that lets the images speak. Simple navigation, with the focus on the visual portfolio so the work convinces through concrete examples.
