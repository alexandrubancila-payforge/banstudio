## About the project

JAB Gym Boxing is a boxing club offering structured training programmes and professional coaching for every level — from beginners to experienced boxers.

## What I built

- **An energetic design** that reflects the spirit of the sport
- **Training programmes** presented by level
- **Coach profiles** with experience and certifications
- **A photo gallery** from training sessions and competitions
- **A membership page** with prices and packages
- **Social media integration**

## Approach

A bold design with strong contrast, full-width imagery and impactful typography — everything communicates energy and determination.
