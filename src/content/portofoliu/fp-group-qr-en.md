## About the project

FP GROUP isn't a company but an ecosystem of projects started by one founder: businesses, a charity foundation and community organisations that are legally independent but share one mission. The group's QR card is the page that ties them all together.

## What we built

- **An introduction to the group**: the "About us" text reads as a short summary and opens in full with one tap
- **The projects, by category**: Charity, Business and Social, each with its own banner and a link to its own QR card, including the [Sfânta Maria](/en/portfolio/sfanta-maria-qr-en) and [Style Room](/en/portfolio/style-room-qr-en) cards
- **Room for new projects**, marked "In progress" until they launch
- **The founder's social networks**: Facebook, Instagram and TikTok
- **The same visual system as the other cards**, in the logo's deep burgundy, so they read as one family
- **Romanian and Russian**, adding to the home screen, sharing and a QR code inside the card

## Details

A single HTML page with no framework, hosted on Cloudflare at qr.fpgroup.md. Every card in the group links back here.
