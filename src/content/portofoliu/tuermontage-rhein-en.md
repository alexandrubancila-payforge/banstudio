## About the project

A business card for **L.F. TürMontage Rhein** (Leonid Filipciuc), a door installation company in Germany. The design pairs a dark brown background with gold accents and the company logo, for a look that reads professional and trustworthy.

## What I built

- **A double-sided design** — contact details on one side, the logo on a light background on the other
- **An embedded QR code** — "scan it and you have every detail in one place"
- **A coherent identity** — a gold-and-brown palette and clear typography
- **Print-ready** — the standard 8.5 × 5.5 cm format, at print quality

## Details

A printed business card in the European format, delivered ready for the press.
