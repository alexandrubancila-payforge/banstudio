## About the project

KinetoTerra is a medical rehabilitation and physiotherapy centre with over 13 years of experience, specialising in neurological, trauma, rheumatological, orthopaedic and paediatric treatment.

## What I built

- **Service pages** dedicated to each speciality
- **A team page** with the physiotherapists' profiles
- **A photo gallery** of the centre
- **A medical blog** with informative articles
- **A price list** structured by category
- **Local SEO** optimised for "physiotherapy Chisinau"

## Results

The site brings in relevant organic traffic and generates direct bookings from local searches.
