## About the project

Taxi Hincesti runs a 24/7 taxi dispatch service in the town of Hincesti, covering local and intercity journeys. The client needed an online presence so customers could find them easily.

## What I built

- **Two domains** — taxihincesti.md and taxihincesti.com for maximum reach
- **Click-to-call** for instant booking from a phone
- **Local SEO** optimised for "taxi Hincesti"
- **An interactive map** of the coverage area
- **A mobile-first design** — most customers arrive from a phone
