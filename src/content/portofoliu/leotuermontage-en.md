## About the project

L.F. TürMontage Rhein is Leonid Filipciuc's company in Dorsheim, in the Rhine region. It fits interior, sliding and front doors, lays laminate and skirting boards, hangs wallpaper and paints. Its customers speak German, Romanian or Russian, so the site had to win all of them over just as quickly and take them straight to a phone call or WhatsApp. We also designed the [business card](/en/portfolio/tuermontage-rhein-en) for the same client.

## What we built

- **Three full languages**: German, Romanian and Russian, with translated addresses for every page, hreflang and one sitemap
- **8 service pages**: interior doors, sliding and glass doors, front doors, door replacement and adjustment, laminate, skirting, wallpaper and painting, each saying what the job includes
- **Call and WhatsApp in one tap**, at the top of the page and in the header, with the WhatsApp message already filled in
- **A link to the company's digital business card**, in a quiet bar above the menu
- **Impressum and Datenschutz** in all three languages, as German law requires
- **Structured data** for Google: a home and construction business with its service catalogue

## Technology

A static site built with Astro: 33 pages, no database and no plugins, quick to load on a phone, even on a building site.
