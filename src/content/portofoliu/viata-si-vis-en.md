## About the project

Viața și Vis is a Romanian nonprofit that offers financial support to children who have ambition but not the material means, helping them pursue their dreams through education.

## What I built

- **The mission presented** through emotional storytelling
- **A projects page** featuring the children who benefit
- **A volunteering section** for recruitment
- **Donation integration** — a visible, accessible donate button
- **A warm design** that inspires trust and empathy

## Impact

The site makes online donations and volunteer recruitment easy, raising the organisation's visibility online.
