## About the project

Spiridon Vangheli is one of Moldova's most important writers. The digital platform was created to promote his literary legacy and make his work accessible to a wide audience.

## What I built

- **An online shop** for buying the author's books
- **An interactive biography** with a timeline and photo gallery
- **A works section** with detailed descriptions
- **A blog** for news and cultural events
- **A responsive design** that respects the literary tone of the content

## Technologies

A multi-page site with e-commerce functionality, optimised for performance and SEO. An elegant design that puts the cultural content first.
