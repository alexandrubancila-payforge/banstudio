## About the project

Anastasia Solomon is a clinical psychologist and psychotherapist in Chisinau with 7 years of experience. The website is a digital business card that presents her therapeutic approach and makes booking a session easy.

## What I built

- **A warm, calming design** — a single page that conveys safety and trust
- **Bilingual (RO / RU)** with a built-in language switcher
- **Clear sections** — About me, Qualifications, Sessions and FAQ
- **Simple booking** with quick contact buttons (WhatsApp, Viber, Telegram) and a form
- **SEO optimisation** and fast loading, tuned for mobile

## Technologies

A one-page business-card website, responsive, optimised for performance and SEO.
