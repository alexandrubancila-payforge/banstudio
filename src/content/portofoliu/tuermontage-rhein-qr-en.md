## About the project

Leonid Filipciuc works on site, at his customers' homes. The digital business card does the job of a stack of printed ones: the customer scans the QR code and has all of the company's details on their phone. It goes with the [printed business card](/en/portfolio/tuermontage-rhein-en) and the [company website](/en/portfolio/leotuermontage-en).

## What we built

- **A big call button**, the first thing under the company name
- **Save to contacts**: a vCard file with the name, phone, email and website that goes into the phone's address book in one tap
- **WhatsApp, email, Facebook and website**, one line each
- **A QR code inside the card**, so it can be shown straight from the phone to a new customer
- **The company's look**: cream, dark brown and gold, the same as the printed card and the website
- **German text**, the language of its customers in Germany

## Details

A single lightweight HTML page at qr.leotuermontage.de. The company website links to it from a bar above the menu.
